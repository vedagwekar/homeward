# Homeward — 60-Second Demo Video

## Important Notes

- **Purpose:** A one-minute app demonstration for the Stellar hackathon presentation. Keep the tone confident, clear, and understated; the accompanying live pitch handles the broader problem and market evidence.
- **Current app status:** The Homes dashboard, Dad's roof repairs project, funding screen, materials evidence, timeline, certificates, and Money back screen are visible in the current prototype. The **Builder**, **Engineer**, and **Parents** workflows are not yet functional in the provided screenshots. Their shots below represent the intended experience and should be shown only if implemented; otherwise use a clearly labelled conceptual sequence.
- **Simulation disclosure:** This is a **Stellar Testnet demo**. Interac, INR payouts, SMS, engineer licences, and sample images are simulated. The exchange rate shown in the funding screen is a demo rate, not a live quote.
- **On-chain accuracy:** The **Stellar Expert** certificate link appears in the UI, but a screenshot alone does not verify a completed transaction or enforceable contract logic. Only show or narrate a successful/blocked on-chain release if its actual testnet behavior has been verified. Do not suggest money has been paid to an Indian bank account.
- **Editing style:** Use screen recordings, clean cuts, restrained text callouts, legible zooms, and quiet background music. Keep the video at **60 seconds total**.

## Voiceover Script

**0:00–0:07 — Introduction**  
“Sending money home is easy. Knowing whether that money becomes real progress is harder.”

**0:07–0:16 — Project dashboard**  
“Homeward organizes construction funding by stage. Families can see what’s been paid, what remains locked, and what still needs funding.”

**0:16–0:25 — Funding**  
“Relatives abroad can contribute to specific stages, with a shared budget and clear costs before they commit.”

**0:25–0:39 — Builder and engineer**  
“Builders submit evidence of completed work. An independent engineer reviews it against the agreed requirements. If a stage fails inspection, the proposed workflow keeps its payment locked until the issue is resolved.”

**0:39–0:50 — Stellar and records**  
“Stellar provides the testnet foundation for recorded decisions and rule-based payments. Families can also review inspection evidence and stage certificates.”

**0:50–1:00 — Family control and closing**  
“And if plans change, Homeward shows how unspent funds can be returned. It’s a clearer way to build a family home from abroad.”
