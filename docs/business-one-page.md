# Homeward: the business on one page

Draft, 13 Nov 2026. For the trifold and the pitch. Every number is either sourced from our evidence files or marked as our own scenario. Nothing here is validated pricing.

## Who pays first

An adult living abroad who is paying, from savings, for a build or repair on their family's own plot in Kerala, with a parent or sibling on site and their own builder. We start with families sending from Canada and the Gulf.

Why Kerala: the Kerala Migration Survey 2023 reports ₹2,16,893 crore of remittances into the state, and lists renovation of houses and shops as the largest use, at 15.8% of reported uses (evidence E04, P2-Q08). That is context for the size of the corridor, not our addressable market.

## What Homeward charges

| Line | Price | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Homeward fee | 1.5% of money locked, charged once when it is funded | Built into the contract and shown on the pay screen |
| Engineer visit | Fixed price quoted up front, the same whether the engineer approves or rejects, never paid through the builder | Policy set; no engineer quotes yet |
| Share of the transfer partner's fee | Unknown | An idea to explore, not a plan |

## One home, worked through (our scenario, not a quote)

| | ₹15 lakh build | ₹30 lakh build |
|---|---|---|
| Homeward fee at 1.5% | ₹22,500 | ₹45,000 |
| Six engineer visits at ₹10,000 | ₹60,000 | ₹60,000 |
| Total cost to the family | ₹82,500 | ₹1,05,000 |
| As a share of the budget | 5.5% | 3.5% |
| Homeward revenue before costs | ₹22,500 | ₹45,000 |

What this tells us: the engineer visits, not our fee, are most of what the family pays, so the service works best on builds of ₹30 lakh and up. On smaller jobs we would need fewer visits or a fixed package.

A hundred fully funded ₹15 lakh builds would bring in ₹22.5 lakh in fees before any costs. We have not estimated support, engineer vetting, payment partner or customer acquisition costs yet.

## Why us and not what exists

| | Hire an engineer | RECONPRO, NRI Land Check | Homeward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent stage check | Yes | Advertised | Yes |
| Several family members fund one plan | No | Not shown publicly | Yes |
| Parent can require a second engineer | No | Not shown publicly | Yes |
| Payment cannot leave without the approvals | No | Not shown publicly | Yes, enforced by the contract |
| Public record of each release | No | No | Yes, on Stellar |

DiasporaBuild sells a similar service to African diaspora families (Part 3, R17). We read that as a sign the need exists in other corridors.

## What we know and what we don't

| Question | Where we stand |
|---|---|
| Is the problem real? | Yes: court and commission cases, and one interview with a father who built from Canada |
| Do people pay for checking? | Yes, for inspections and oversight |
| Will they pay 1.5% plus visits for Homeward? | Unknown. This is what the pilot tests |
| Can we hold real money? | Not yet. It needs a licensed payment partner under RBI rules |
