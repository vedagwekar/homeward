# Engineer quotes for video reviews

Collected by the team in November 2026. Names are left out of this public repo; the team keeps them. The prices have not been checked against wider Kerala rates.

A "review" is about 20 minutes at a desk: the engineer watches the builder's stage video and passes or fails it. The engineer is paid the same either way.

Range: ₹500 to ₹1,200 per review.

## What this changes

**The price to the family gets simpler.** The 1.5% fee now covers the engineer's video reviews: six on a ₹15 lakh build, seven on a ₹30 lakh build. Before, we assumed visits at ₹10,000 each on top of the fee.

**A video is not enough for every stage.** Steel size, spacing and concrete cover can't be confirmed from a video, and two of the eight asked for close-ups or a tape measure in shot. For the stages concrete will hide (foundation and roof slab), the engineer visits the site. The team's estimate for a site visit is ₹2,000 to ₹3,000 plus travel. **That figure is an estimate, not one of these quotes.**

## What is left for Homeward, per build

Other costs are the assumptions in `business-case.md` (payment partner 0.25%, identity checks ₹600, support ₹6,000, dispute reserve 10% of the fee, winning the customer ₹5,000).

| | ₹15 lakh build | ₹30 lakh build |
|---|---|---|
| Fee, 1.5% | ₹22,500 | ₹45,000 |
| Video reviews at the average quote | ₹4,388 (6) | ₹6,431 (7) |
| Left after reviews | ₹18,113 | ₹38,569 |
| Other costs (assumptions) | ₹17,600 | ₹23,600 |
| **Left per build** | **about ₹500** | **about ₹15,000** |

With a small team costing ₹30 lakh a year, that is about **200 builds a year on ₹30 lakh builds**, roughly 2.9% of our base estimate of 6,850 Kerala builds. On ₹15 lakh builds the fee barely covers its costs. This replaces the earlier "about 100 builds" figure, which relied on keeping a 15% margin on ₹10,000 visits.

## What would break it

- A real quote above ₹3,750 per review on a ₹15 lakh build, or ₹6,430 on a ₹30 lakh build, leaves nothing after reviews.
- An engineer who won't pass a stage without seeing the site. For foundation and roof slab we already plan a visit.
- Many rejected clips. Each re-film costs one more review.
