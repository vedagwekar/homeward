What we have learned

16 conversations: 15 engineers (Mumbai 6, Delhi 5, Kerala 2, Canada 2) and a father who built from Canada.

"Easy to send money, very, very hard to get money back."

Krish's father, quoted with permission

Engineers said it would save them hours. None quoted a price, and we haven't tested the fee on families yet.

Break-even

About 100 builds a year on ₹30 lakh builds with a small team: roughly 1.4% of our Kerala estimate. Fee alone on ₹15 lakh builds would need about 27%, so we don't pitch that. Every input is our assumption.

Still to prove
