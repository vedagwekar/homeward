We would rather you hear them from us. Put a dot on the one that would stop you backing us.

Will families actually pay?

Not tested yet

What we have

16 conversations: 15 engineers (Mumbai, Delhi, Kerala, Canada) and 1 father who built from Canada. Three consumer-commission cases.

What we don't

Engineers back it, but they would gain clients. We have not yet shown families the fee.

Is the real price too high?

Partly answered

What we have

Moving money costs families 4.4% to 6.5% by bank, 1% to 1.5% by digital service. Adding Homeward and visits: about 4% to 7% all in by digital, 7.5% to 12% by bank.

What we don't

Whether that is worth it to them.

3

Can the contract charge for everything?

Partly answered

What we have

The contract charges a fixed 1.5%. In the pilot, engineer visits and a minimum fee go through a payment partner, off-chain.

What we don't

Visits on-chain, flat fees and monthly plans. They need a new contract version.

Next

Publish the visit schedule and our margin, so nobody profits from extra visits in secret.

4

Does the business add up?

Partly answered

What we have

On ₹30 lakh builds with a small team, about 100 builds a year, roughly 1.4% of our base estimate for Kerala.

What we don't

Every input is our assumption. On ₹15 lakh builds with the fee alone, we would need about 27% of the market, so we don't pitch that.

6

Will builders accept it?

Not tested yet

What we have

Terms are set per build: the deposit can be zero or earned from the first payment, and the held-back share is capped at 20%. Engineers told us what builders need: agreed materials advances and fixed response times.

What we don't

No builder has said yes.

Next

Builder calls.

7

Who holds the money, legally?

Not tested yet

What we have

We don't hold rupees. The first pilot is noncustodial: families pay from their own bank after each approval.

What we don't

No partner signed. No legal advice on cross-border rules yet. Holding money needs a licensed partner under RBI rules.

Next

Partner and counsel conversations.

8

Who settles a dispute?

Partly answered

What we have

Today our admin account settles a contested report. It cannot send money to itself. Try to break it at this table.

What we don't

That is a trusted step. Dispute costs and legal risk are not yet in our numbers.

Next

An independent arbiter, named in each build, before real money.

How do we find families?

Partly answered

What we have

Through engineers, at the moment a family signs a builder's quote. Engineers told us Homeward would save them hours of chasing approvals.

What we don't

Our ₹5,000 cost to win a family is a guess.

Isn't a relative and WhatsApp enough?
